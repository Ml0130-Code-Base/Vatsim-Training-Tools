Set the sector, build the drill, fly it, bank what it taught.

Kalamazoo ATCT works as Great Lakes Approach, the West Wall of the Great Lakes TRACON. The sectors, the departure areas, the inter-sector altitudes and the ZAU interface are carried from the vZAU documents, and the TRACON footprint from the ZAU vARTCC's own sector data. The West Wall sector boundaries and the published tracks are still not carried, and every empty slot says so and names the document that fills it.

Skeleton. Nothing here is invented: a number is either traced to a vZAU document by paragraph, or the slot is empty and marked empty.
Skeleton tool. Single file, no build step, no network, works from file://. Every operational number traces to the vZAU document set — see claude_Source_Documents_Index.md and claude_AZO_Reference.md.

TRACON footprint and field positions from the VATGlasses ZAU dataset, managed by ZAU vARTCC — vatglasses.uk, licensed CC BY-NC-SA 4.0. Second-tier data: the facility orders win over it, and an owner-supplied boundary supersedes it.
