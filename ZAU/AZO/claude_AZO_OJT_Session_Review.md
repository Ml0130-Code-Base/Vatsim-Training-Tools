## How to fill this in after session 1

1. **Source self-check**: what is verified, which sessions are covered, whether the notes are contemporaneous.
2. **Status grid** by pattern and session. A pattern earns a row when it appears twice.
3. **One section per pattern**: what happened (callsign, moment), the mechanism, the citation (SOP, ZAU LOA or ZOB LOA paragraph), the confidence caveat, the standing item and its `id`.
4. **Then, and only then**, add the items to `DD.STANDING` and tag every grading call with the id.
