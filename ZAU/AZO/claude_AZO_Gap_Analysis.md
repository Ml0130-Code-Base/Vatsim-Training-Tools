# AZO Gap Analysis — what the documents do not answer

Assembled 2026-10-28 from `claude_AZO_Reference.md` (vZAU Great Lakes TRACON SOP 7110.65A eff. 2025-01-09; ZAU-AZO ATCT LOA eff. 2025-12-21; vZAU-vZOB AZO ATCT LOA eff. 2020-12-13), `claude_AZO_CIFP_Procedures.md` (CIFP 261001), the ZAU vNAS record and the community footprint.

**The headline: the West Wall boundaries are pictures, and CIFP codes no SID or STAR at any of the six fields.** Appendices A and B of the SOP and Appendix A of the ZAU LOA are chart images. VATGlasses gives one polygon, surface to FL100, covering both walls.

This file records facts about what is missing; work items are in the issue tracker.

---

## Part 1 — What the owner has offered to supply, ranked by training value

### 1. The six West Wall sector boundaries

Pictures (SOP Appendices A and B, ZAU LOA Appendix A, `vZAU TRACON Boundaries.pdf`). vNAS lists an `AZO SECTORS` map (56 lines) that has been pulled and **not read as sectors**. *"Is the AZO SECTORS video map the West Wall split?"* is a one-line answer.

### 2. The AZO TRSA lateral and vertical limits

SOP 4-3 requires TRSA service and the TRSA is never depicted. Charts at charts.zauartcc.org.

### 3. Which beacon block is current

The LOA blocks (`0200-0277, 4200-4577, 5500-5537`) and the SOP's West Wall VFR block (`4500-4577 and 5500-5537`) disagree. The tool carries both. Whichever document is superseded closes it.

### 4. The near limit in SOP 3-5

The text reads *"no closer than flying miles from the airport"*: a word is missing. A corrected copy of the SOP closes it.

### 5. Tower hours and Class D dimensions for AZO, BTL, GRR and MKG

`vZAU Minor Fields SOP 7110.65B`, not yet read.

### 6. KHLM coordinates

Park Township is in neither the VATGlasses dataset nor CIFP. It carries no coordinates, runways or procedures. Public airport data or a chart would close it.

## Part 4 — Conflicts and as-written anomalies, preserved

1. **CAPITAL / Capitol**: spelled both ways across the SOP, Appendix B and the ZAU LOA.
2. **Beacon blocks**: LOA versus SOP, as above.
3. **The 3-5 transfer-of-control sentence** with the missing word.
4. **VATGlasses models three AZO positions** where the orders define six; vNAS carries all six with the orders' callsigns and frequencies, so the community dataset is the short one.
5. **Kalamazoo's runways**: the order's departure-area pairs leave out runways 5 and 23, which CIFP carries. Both are printed.
