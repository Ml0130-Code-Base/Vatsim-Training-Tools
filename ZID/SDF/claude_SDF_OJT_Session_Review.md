## The rule this file enforces

> **A standing training item is an observed shortfall, not a document-derived guess.**

M98's five standing items came from watching one controller work one metroplex. They are facts about M98 sessions, not facts about terminal control, and importing them here would fabricate a training history. `SDF.STANDING` is an empty array, the smoke test asserts it, and the tool says so on the notes page. **Leave it empty until a session is flown.**

## How to fill this in after session 1

Follow the M98 review's shape: a source self-check; a status grid (pattern by session - a pattern earns a row when it appears twice); one section per pattern with what happened, the mechanism, the citation (which chapter of the SDF SOP the correct action comes from), a confidence caveat, and the standing item it becomes with a short id. Then, and only then, add the derived items to `STANDING` and tag every grading call with the id it exercises.
