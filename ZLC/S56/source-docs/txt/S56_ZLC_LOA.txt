1 . PURPOSE:This Letter of Agreement establishesprocedures for handling air traffic between
S alt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center (ZLC ARTCC) and Salt Lake City Terminal Radar
Approach Control (S56 TRACON).

2. SCOPE:T o establish standard operating proceduresfor the coordination and control of
aircraft between ZLC ARTCC and S56 TRACON on the VATSIM network. This letter of
a greement is supplemental to vZLC Facility Policy and FAA Order JO 7110.65. The information
c ontained herein is to be used for flight simulation purposes only on the VATSIM network. It is
n ot intended, nor should it be used for, real-world navigation. The Virtual Salt Lake City ARTCC
is not affiliated with the FAA, the actual Salt Lake City ARTCC, or any governing aviation body.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES:

    a . All controllers staffing (or controlling overlying airspace containing) ZLC ARTCC and/or
         S 56 TRACON positions are required to be familiar with the provisions of this document.

    b . ZLC ARTCC delegates responsibility to S56 TRACON for providing terminal air traffic
         control services within the airspace depicted in Attachment 1.

    a . North Flow: Operations at SLC utilizing Runways 34L, 34R and 35.

    b. S outh Flow: Operations at SLC utilizing Runways 16L, 16R and 17.

         etc.) that, after crossing defined fixes, will remain constant. For unlike types, the spacing
         w ill be accomplished when the succeeding aircraft crosses the defined fix.

         NOTE-
         T his definition assumes that the receiving facility issues no control instructions.

         N OTE-
         F or departures (from S56 to ZLC), MIT will be maintained until the succeeding aircraft
         c rosses the common boundary.

5. GENERAL:

    a. W hen SLC ATCT advises S56 TRACON of a runway change, S56 TRACON must notify
         ZLC ARTCC of the runway change. Coordination must include, but is not limited to:

              (1) T he number of remaining departures in the old configuration and the callsign of
                   the last departure assigned the old departure routing.

              (2) T he callsign of the last arrival that will be accepted on the old arrival routing.

7 . DEPARTURES:

    a . S56 TRACON must:

              (1) P rovide ZLC ARTCC with 5 MIT between successive departures, constant or
                   increasing, unless transitional separation (see 7.c.) is being used.

              (2) Ensure departing aircraft enter ZLC ARTCC airspace at, or climbing to, the
                   appropriate altitude (or lower filed altitude) in accordance with Attachment 3.

    b. Z LC ARTCC must have control for the following within 20 NM of the common boundary:

              (1) C limb to filed altitude.

              (2) Turns out of the gate, provided the turns do not exceed 30° either side of the
                   previous course.

    c. T ransitional Separation:

              (1) W hen transitioning from terminal to enroute control, separation as described in
                   FAA Order JO 7110.65 Paragraph 5-5-4.d.4. is authorized between departing
                   aircraft on diverging routes/courses that will enter ZLC ARTCC airspace.

              (2) S56 TRACON must establish aircraft on the diverging routes/courses prior to the
                   c ommon boundary and prior to communications transfer.

              (3) T ransitional separation must only be utilized between aircraft departing on the
                   following routes:

                        (a) ZIONZ#.ROMMN/FFU133R and ZIONZ#.LODUY/FFU170R.

                        (b) CGULL#/TCH296R and DEZRT#/V32/250° heading.

                        (c) A RCHZ#/SEVYR#/TCH193R and TCH182R.

                        (d) A ll SANDY and BRIGHTON gate departures.

7. HOLDING:

    a . P rimary holding will normally be accomplished at the holding fixes of BVL, LHO,
         JAMMN, and SPANE. When ZLC is instructed to hold, S56 will normally accept handoffs
         on all aircraft inside the fixes listed above and within S56 airspace.

    b . When S56 requests holding from ZLC sector 32 and/or 11, S56 must release the
         JAMMN and SPANE Shelves. ZLC will return the airspace to S56 when it is no longer
         n eeded for holding.

    c. S TACO Holding Plan Procedures

              (1) S56 TMU/CIC serves as the focal point between ZLC TMU, ZLC Sector 40, and
                   all affected S56 sectors for execution of the STACO holding plane. S56 TMU will
                   ensure coordination with all impacted S56 sectors to confirm protection of the
                   d esignated airspace.

              (2) A ll aircraft held in the STACO Holding Plan must be on ZLC Sector 40 Frequency
                   and held at or above FL190

              (3) U pon Notification by S56 TMU/CIC to ZLC TMU to initiate the STACO Holding
                   P lan, all S56 airspace underlying ZLC Sector 40 is released to ZLC Sector 40 at
                   and above FL190.

8 . OVERFLIGHT AIRCRAFT (EVENT ONLY):

A ll overflight traffic at or above 15,000 feet MSL, along normal active jet arrival flows do not
require verbal coordination with the exception of HCR arrivals. All other traffic must be verbally
c oordinated on an individual basis no later than 5 minutes prior to entering S56 airspace.

H EBER ARRIVALS: Aircraft transitioning S56 airspace must enter through the BIRD, DELLE, or
P AYSON gates. ZLC must assign FL210 and direct TCH..CUVAK..HCR. S56 must hand-off
a ircraft to ZLC Sector 03 CUVAK..HCR and assign 14,000. ZLC Sector 03 has control for speed
a djustments, turns up to 30 degrees, and descent.
