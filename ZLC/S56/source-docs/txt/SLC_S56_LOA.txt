Salt Lake City Terminal Radar Approach Control and Salt Lake City Airport Traffic Control Tower

                             LETTER OF AGREEMENT

                                        EFFECTIVE: November 7, 2025

SUBJECT: Coordination Procedures

1. PURPOSE: This Letter of Agreement establishes procedures for handling air traffic between
Salt Lake City Terminal Radar Approach Control (S56 TRACON) and Salt Lake City Airport
Traffic Control Tower (SLC ATCT).

2. SCOPE: To establish standard operating procedures for the coordination and control of
aircraft between S56 TRACON and SLC ATCT on the VATSIM network. This letter of agreement
is supplemental to vZLC Facility Policy and FAA Order JO 7110.65. The information contained
herein is to be used for flight simulation purposes only on the VATSIM network. It is not
intended, nor should it be used for, real-world navigation. The Virtual Salt Lake City ARTCC is
not affiliated with the FAA, the actual Salt Lake City ARTCC, or any governing aviation body.

3. DEFINITIONS:

    a. Runway Flow: The primary direction of landing and departing traffic.

    b. North Flow: Operations at Salt Lake City International Airport (SLC) utilizing Runways
         34L, 34R, 35, and 32.

    c. South Flow: Operations at SLC utilizing Runways 16L, 16R, 17, and 14.

    d. Runway Use Plan (RUP): A plan that outlines procedures applicable to the use of
         individual runways.

    e. Departure Plan: A plan that outlines procedures authorizing the application of
         pilot-provided visual separation for successive departures during certain weather
         conditions.

    f. North Flow straight-out departures: SLC IFR departures that fly a 310° or 340° heading.

    g. South Flow straight-out departures: SLC IFR departures that fly a 160° heading.

    h. Turn departures: SLC IFR departures other than straight-outs.

    i. Unrestricted VFR (UVFR): A VFR Category III turbojet aircraft which joins the SLC IFR
         departure flow.

    j. BARN Transition: A bi-directional route between the BARN VFR waypoint, the approach
         end of Runway 16R, and the approach end of Runway 17.

    k. I-80 Transition: A bi-directional route between a point over I-80 and abeam the KSL
         antenna, the approach end of Runway 34L, and the approach end of Runway 35.

4. RESPONSIBILITIES:

    a. All controllers staffing (or controlling overlying airspace containing) S56 TRACON and/or
         SLC ATCT positions are required to be familiar with the provisions of this document.

    b. S56 TRACON delegates to SLC ATCT that airspace as depicted in Attachment 1
         extending from the surface up to and including 8,500 feet MSL.

    c. S56 TRACON delegates control and responsibility to SLC ATCT for separation of aircraft
         within airspace delegated to SLC ATCT and on final approach beginning at the
         delegated airspace boundary.

5. GENERAL:

    a. Each SLC ATCT Local Control (LC) position must Quick-Look the S56 TRACON Final,
         Valley, Bear, Lake, Gunnison, Stockton, and Jordan positions.

    b. Each LC position must set the TDW to display, at a minimum, 7 miles from the final
         approach fix of the runway in use, and at least 7 miles from the departure end of the
         runway.

    c. SLC ATCT may operate to the lateral boundary between SLC ATCT airspace and S56
         TRACON airspace. S56 TRACON sectors must ensure that aircraft under their control
         remain the appropriate distance from SLC ATCT airspace.

    d. When it has been determined an aircraft cleared for approach to SLC will be broken off
         the approach, the controller must coordinate with appropriate positions to ensure
         separation and transfer of control to S56 TRACON positions.

              (1) Except to prevent a loss of separation or during an emergency situation, aircraft
                   broken off the final must be instructed to track the final approach course until the
                   approach end of the runway, or fly a straight-out heading (if on a visual
                   approach).

6. DEPARTURE PLANS:

    a. Definitions:

              (1) Visual Departure Plan (Plan V, V+, or VN): Weather allows the application of
                   visual separation until routes diverge.

              (2) Instrument Departure Plan (Plan I): Weather precludes the application of visual
                   separation until routes diverge.

    b. Responsibilities:
              (1) SLC ATCT must keep S56 TRACON informed of the departure plan in use.
              (2) S56 TRACON must advise SLC ATCT if weather conditions prohibit the use of
                   visual separation.

    c. Procedures:
              (1) Visual Departure Plan:
                        (a) SLC ATCT must:
                                (i) Verbally coordinate with S56 TRACON when pilot-provided visual
                                       separation is being used.
                               (ii) Not utilize visual separation for aircraft departing to the same gate.
                              (iii) Ensure compliance with FAA JO 7110.65 paragraph 7-2-1.a.2.
                        (b) V Plan: The following routes are authorized for pilot-provided visual
                             separation:
                                (i) DEZRT departure with a CGULL departure.
                               (ii) DEZRT departure with a 280° course to GUNNISON gate.
                              (iii) 280° course to SUNSET gate with a CGULL departure.
                              (iv) 280° course to SUNSET gate with a 280° course to GUNNISON
                                       gate.
                        (c) V+ Plan: Adds the following to the V Plan:
                                (i) EDEN (HOLTR) gate departures.
                               (ii) J154 (SPINE) departures.
                              (iii) J56, J173, TCH094R (HERTS, UPJAR, LOFOG) departures.
                        (d) VN Plan (North Flow only): For use when visual separation is not
                             available on turn departures. Visual separation can be utilized
                             independently in any combination of the following:
                                (i) EDEN (HOLTR) gate departures.
                               (ii) J154 (SPINE) departures.
                              (iii) J56, J173, TCH094R (HERTS, UPJAR, LOFOG) departures.
              (2) Instrument Departure Plan: All aircraft must have proper in-trail spacing.

7. RUNWAY USE PLANS (RUPs):
    a. Definitions:
              (1) Normal Runway RUPs (NRV, NRV+, NRVN, NRI): All runways are available for
                   departures and arrivals.
              (2) Departure-Only RUPs: All runways are available for departures. Some runways
                   are available for arrivals.
                        (a) XXRI, XXRV, XXRV+, or XXRVN Plan:
                                (i) All runways are available for departures.
                               (ii) All runways except Runway 34R are available for arrivals.
                        (b) XXLI, XXLV, or XXLV+ Plan:
                                (i) All runways are available for departures.
                               (ii) All runways except Runway 16L are available for arrivals.
                        (c) XX7I, XX7V, or XX7V+ Plan:
                                (i) All runways are available for departures.
                               (ii) All runways except Runway 17 are available for arrivals.
                        (d) XX5I, XX5V, XX5V+, or XX5VN Plan:
                                (i) All runways are available for departures.
                               (ii) All runways except Runway 35 are available for arrivals.
    b. Responsibilities:
              (1) SLC ATCT must coordinate with S56 TRACON prior to establishing a RUP other
                   than Normal Runway.
    c. Procedures:
              (1) A RUP must always be in use.
              (2) A Departure Plan must always be in use.
                        (a) The selected departure plan is placed at the end of the RUP code (i.e.
                             The Normal Runway RUP with a V+ departure plan is coded "NRV+").
              (3) The Normal Runway RUP must be the default RUP.

8. VFR PROCEDURES:

    a. SLC ATCT must:

              (1) Issue the following items to all SLC VFR departures that will leave SLC ATCT
                   airspace:

                        (a) Class Bravo clearance.

                        (b) Appropriate departure frequency.

                        (c) Discrete beacon code.

              (2) Provide initial separation on VFR departures.

              (3) Transfer communications prior to entering S56 TRACON airspace.

              (4) Radar identify and verify Mode C on all VFR aircraft not routed in the IFR flow.

              (5) Have control on contact for descent and turns toward the airport for VFR arrivals.

              (6) Route southbound VFR aircraft (over-flight or departure) "over", "via", or "follow"
                   I-15 freeway at 5,500, 6,500, 7,500, or 8,500. These aircraft must be considered
                   in Class Bravo airspace until the Valley controller advises or routes the aircraft
                   outside of Class B airspace.

              (7) Route northbound VFR aircraft (over-flight or departure) "over", "via", or "follow"
                   I-15 freeway at 6,000, 7,000, or 8,000.

              (8) Inform VFR aircraft routed northbound when they leave Class Bravo airspace
                   and to resume appropriate VFR altitude.

              (9) Provide radar services to BTF VFR arrivals/departures to/from all directions
                   except north. Assign VFR BTF departures initial routes and altitudes consistent
                   with SLC VFR flow.

              (10) Coordinate requests for VFR aircraft requesting practice approaches at SLC
                   with the appropriate S56 TRACON sector(s).

b. S56 TRACON must:

         (1) Transfer communications prior to entering SLC ATCT airspace, but no sooner
              than 7 miles from the lateral boundary of SLC ATCT airspace.

         (2) Have control of VFR aircraft upon communications transfer.

         (3) Assign a Class Bravo clearance to all arrivals and overflights prior to
              communications transfer to SLC ATCT.

              (4) Route VFR Category I, Category II, and Category III turboprop aircraft as follows:

                        (a) Landing SLC:

                                (i) From the north: Over I-15 at 6,500.

                                       NOTE-
                                       SLC ATCT is responsible for separation between the arrival and
                                       the lower northbound traffic.

                               (ii) From the south: Over I-15 at 6,000.

                              (iii) From the west (North Flow): Via I-80 Transition at 6,500.

                              (iv) From the west (South Flow): Via BARN Transition at 6,500.

                               (v) From the east: Over I-80 to I-15 descending to (or at) 9,000.

                        (b) Overflights:

                                (i) From the north transitioning southbound: Via I-15 at 6,500, 7,500,
                                       or 8,500.

                               (ii) From the south transitioning northbound: Via I-15 at 6,000, 7,000,
                                       or 8,000.

                              (iii) From the west transitioning eastbound (North Flow): Via I-80
                                       Transition at 6,500.

                              (iv) From the west transitioning eastbound (South Flow): Via BARN
                                       Transition at 6,500.

                               (v) From the east transitioning westbound (North Flow): Via I-80
                                       Transition at 6,000.

                              (vi) From the east transitioning westbound (South Flow): Via BARN
                                       Transition at 6,000.

              (5) Provide radar services to BTF VFR arrivals/departures to/from the north.

         NOTE-
         None of the above clauses listed in 6.a. or 6.b. may apply to UVFR departures.

9. ARRIVALS:

    a. Reduced separation on final is authorized on all runways, except Runway 14/32, in
         accordance with FAA JO 7110.65 paragraph 5-5-4.

    b. SLC ATCT must:

              (1) Separate IFR/VFR arrivals from the SLC ATCT airspace boundary to the airport.

              (2) Coordinate with the appropriate S56 TRACON sector for aircraft conducting a
                   missed approach or go-around. Issue the turn or straight-out climb as
                   appropriate. Aircraft must not turn prior to the departure end of the runway.

                        (a) Turn:

                                (i) North Flow: Heading 280, altitude 9,000, speed 210. No prominent
                                       obstacles are identified.

                               (ii) South Flow: Heading 280, altitude 9,000, speed 210. No
                                       prominent obstacles are identified.

                        (b) Straight-out:

                                (i) North Flow: Heading 340, altitude 9,000, speed 210. No prominent
                                       obstacles are identified.

                               (ii) South Flow: Heading 160, altitude 9,000, speed 210. No
                                       prominent obstacles are identified.

                   NOTE-
                   Provide five miles in-trail with a subsequent straight-out departure following a
                   "Straight-out" climb out. Provide five miles in-trail with a subsequent turn
                   departure following a "Turn" climb-out.

              (3) Issue climb out instructions consistent with normal VFR departure routings (as
                   specified in 8.a.) to aircraft executing VFR practice approaches.

    c. SLC ATCT has control:

              (1) To clear aircraft for a visual approach if the aircraft is 9 miles or less from the
                   runway. This must be to the previously assigned runway and the appropriate S56
                   TRACON sector must be verbally advised.

              (2) To change the assigned runway of arrivals established on final within the lateral
                   boundaries of SLC ATCT airspace.

    d. S56 TRACON must:
              (1) Transfer communications prior to entering SLC ATCT airspace, but no sooner
                   than 7 miles from the lateral boundary of SLC ATCT airspace.
              (2) Ensure scratchpad entries are entered in accordance with Attachment 3 prior to
                   communications transfer to SLC ATCT.
              (3) Obtain approval from the appropriate LC prior to authorizing an approach that will
                   not terminate in a full stop. For those aircraft that will depart IFR, assign 160/340
                   heading (whichever will result in a straight-out) and maintain 8,000.

10. DEPARTURES:
    a. SLC ATCT must:
              (1) Provide initial separation on all departures routed in the IFR flow.
              (2) Issue all IFR departures the restriction: "Maintain 9,000."
              (3) Issue all turbojet departures the restriction: "Maintain 240 knots."
              (4) Issue VFR Category III turbojet aircraft (UVFR departures) a clearance out of
                   Class Bravo airspace, assign an altitude of 8,500, and a departure frequency in
                   accordance with the IFR flow.
              (5) Call for release on all of the following:
                        (a) Aircraft landing at PVU, SVR, TVY, BTF, OGD, HIF, BMC, LGU, or HCR.
                        (b) Aircraft issued an initial altitude other than 9,000.
                        (c) Turbojet aircraft not issued a 240 knot speed restriction.
                        (d) Aircraft not assigned a DP.
              (6) Automatic Departure Releases:
                        (a) Are granted to SLC ATCT for aircraft departing the runways in use.
                        (b) Are canceled during a runway change or when advised by S56 TRACON.
              (7) Ensure the following IFR/UVFR departure headings:
                        (a) North Flow:
                                (i) EDEN and SANDY gates: 340°
                               (ii) BIRD gate and HIF/OGD/BMC arrivals: 310°
                              (iii) GUNNISON gate (prop and turbo-prop only): 295°

                              (iv) SUNSET gate and GUNNISON gate (jet only): 280°
                               (v) PANEL, PAYSON, and OQUIRRH gates: 260°
                        (b) South Flow:
                                (i) BRIGHTON, PAYSON, HOBBLE, and PANEL gates: 160°
                               (ii) GUNNISON and SUNSET gates: 280°
                              (iii) BIRD, EDEN, and SANDY gates and HIF/OGD/BMC arrivals: 295°

11. OPPOSITE DIRECTION OPERATIONS (ODO):
    a. Definitions:
              (1) ODO: IFR/VFR operations conducted to the same or parallel runway where an
                   aircraft is operating in a reciprocal direction of another aircraft arriving, departing,
                   or conducting an approach.
              (2) Same Runway: The same piece of concrete; 16L/34R, 16R/34L, 17/35, or 14/32.
              (3) Parallel Runway: A piece of pavement that parallels another. At SLC, all three
                   north/south facing runways are considered parallel to each other. Runway 14/32
                   does not fall under this categorization.
              (4) Cutoff Point: The ODO cutoff for IFR/IFR or VFR aircraft receiving IFR services,
                   north or south flow, is a 15-mile final.
    b. Responsibilities:
              (1) ODOs must only be conducted if there is an operational necessity.

              (2) Initiation of ODO must be verbally coordinated.

              (3) SLC ATCT and S56 TRACON share the responsibility of issuing traffic advisories
                   to participating aircraft.

              (4) S56 TRACON must issue control instructions to protect the integrity of the cutoff
                   point if the conditions for conducting ODO are not met.

    c. Procedures:

              (1) Same Runway Opposite Direction Arrival/Departure:

                        (a) Prior to an opposite direction arrival reaching a 15-mile final, SLC ATCT
                             must ensure the opposite direction departure is airborne and established
                             on a heading that differs by at least 45° from the reciprocal of the inbound
                             course of the arrival.

                        (b) IFR separation must be maintained at all times. Visual separation is not
                             authorized.

              (2) Parallel Runway Opposite Direction Arrival/Departure:

                        (a) Flight paths do not cross:

                                (i) SLC ATCT must ensure the departure aircraft is issued a heading
                                       that turns away from an opposite direction arrival that is inside a
                                       15-mile final to the other runway.

                               (ii) Once a turn away from opposing traffic is issued, the use of visual
                                       separation is authorized.

                        (b) Flight paths cross:

                                (i) Prior to an opposite direction arrival reaching a 15-mile final, SLC
                                       ATCT must ensure the opposite direction departure is airborne,
                                       has crossed the path of, and is established on a heading that
                                       differs by at least 45° from the reciprocal of the inbound course of
                                       the arrival.

                               (ii) Once flight paths have crossed, use of visual separation is
                                       authorized.

              (3) Same or Parallel Runway Opposite Direction Arrival/Arrival:

                        (a) S56 TRACON must ensure that the first arrival has landed before the
                             second arrival reaches a 15-mile final.

                        (b) SLC ATCT must advise S56 TRACON when the first arrival has landed.
