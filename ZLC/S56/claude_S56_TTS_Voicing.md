# S56 Voicing for Text-to-Speech

The owner listens to drill responses via text-to-speech. **TTS reads exactly what is written.** Table is WRITTEN identifier → spoken form. Where no spoken form is listed, write and speak as-is.

Same contract as `../../ZMP/R90/claude_R90_TTS_Voicing.md`. Callsign digit grouping, registration spelling and "zero, never oh" live in `claude_US_Carrier_Callsigns.md` (copied at `../_shared/`).

## Sector names and callsigns

Radio name **"Salt Lake City Approach"**. Sector names (Lake, Gunnison, Bear, Hart, Final, Jordan, Stockton, Valley, Alpine) are English words, spoken as written. Callsigns are `SLC_<letter>_APP`; speak the letter as a letter. Salt Lake Center sector 3 is `SLC_03_CTR`.

`Antelope`, `Keyhole`, `Jepson`, `Avon`, `Bird`, `Heber` are English words and are spoken as written. `P2`, `P3`, `P4` are spoken "P two", "P three", "P four".

## Runways

Expanded: `34L` → "three four left", `16R` → "one six right", `17` → "one seven". Never "thirty-four left".

## Frequencies

Digit by digit with "point": `135.500` → "one three five point five zero zero". Never "one thirty-five".

## What to do when a pronunciation is missing

Write the identifier as-is, and add the row here when the owner supplies it.
