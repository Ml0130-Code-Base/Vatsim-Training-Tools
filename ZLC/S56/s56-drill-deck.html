S56 — skeleton

Nine sectors, three roots, and somewhere to put what you learn.

The selector is built and the frequencies are verified. The engine is not. Every sector carries a note slot, because the notes are what the next version gets built from.

Skeleton tool. Sector names, STARS identifiers, VATSIM callsigns and frequencies are verified against S56 TRACON 7110.20C chapter 3 and the ZLC Position and Frequency Info sheet — see claude_S56_Reference.md and ../_shared/claude_ZLC_Positions_Reference.md. Notes live in this browser only; export them before clearing site data. Single file, no build step, no network.
