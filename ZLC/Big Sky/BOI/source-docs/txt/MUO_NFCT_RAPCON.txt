                                                          Local
1. Frequency

       1.1. Mountain Home Tower (Local): 133.850
2. Airspace
       2.1. Local: Runway 12/30, and the airspace within 5NM of the center of the field.
3. Procedures

       3.1. Runway operations
              3.1.1. Use runway 30 for arrivals and departures (West) during calm wind
                           operations.
              3.1.2. Use runway 12 for arrivals and departures (East) when the tailwind
                           component exceeds 5 knots

       3.2. LAHSO
              3.2.1. Not authorized.

       3.3. Tower Use of Radar
              3.3.1. Full

4. Departures
       4.1. Departure Notification / Releases
              4.1.1. Departure Notification are not required, unless otherwise coordinate by
                           RAPCON
              4.1.2. Departures are automatically release unless otherwise coordinate by
                           RAPCON
       4.2. IFR Departures
              4.2.1. Ensure aircraft turn to assigned point/heading and altitude reporting is
                           operating prior to handoff to departure.

5. Arrivals
       5.1. IFR arrival
              5.1.1. Quicklook Radar North
              5.1.2. Accept radar handoff of all IFR arrivals, if radar certified and it is
                           operationally beneficial
       5.2. Missed Approach/Go Around
              5.2.1. Acquire the track control for the aircraft conducting a missed approach.
              5.2.2. Send a Missed Notification (.MA) for the aircraft executing an unplanned
                           Missed Approach/Go Around to the appropriate RAPCON sector that will
                           be receiving the aircraft for re-sequencing.
              5.2.3. VFR aircraft, and/or IFR Category I/II aircraft conducting a visual
                           approach and executing a Go-Around must be instructed to remain in the
                           local pattern, if capable of doing so. The following is considered Primary
                           Climb-Out procedure and does not require any special coordination with
                           Approach Control or Strip marking (Scratchpad entries) other than the
                           basic Missed Notification. Aircraft must not turn prior to the departure end
                           of the runway. Handoff/Comm-Change to the appropriate RAPCON sector
                           must be completed no later than 1 mile off the departure end of the
                           runway.
                      5.2.3.1. PRIMARY CLIMB OUT PROCEDURE:
                             5.2.3.1.1. West Flow: Heading 270, Maintain 5,000.
                             5.2.3.1.2. East Flow: Heading 100, Maintain 5,000.
              5.2.4. VFR Traffic join local pattern

6. VFR Operations
       6.1. VFR Departures
              6.1.1. Depart on course at or below 5,500.
              6.1.2. Handoff to departure control as soon as clear of any possible conflicts
                      6.1.2.1. MUO - 124.8

       6.2. VFR Arrivals
              6.2.1. Direct to airport at or below 5,500.

       6.3. VFR Flight Following
              6.3.1. Ensure all aircraft requiring flight following have the appropriate scratch
                           pad entry prior to handoff to departure.

                 including 16,000.

                           Lake Center.
