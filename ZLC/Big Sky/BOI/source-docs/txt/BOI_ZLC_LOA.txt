                                    LETTER OF AGREEMENT

                                  EFFECTIVE: November 1, 2024

SUBJECT: Coordination Procedures

1. PURPOSE: This Letter of Agreement establishes procedures for handling air traffic between
Salt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center (ZLC ARTCC) and Boise Airport Traffic Control
Tower/Terminal Radar Approach Control (herein referred to as "Big Sky TRACON").

2. SCOPE: To establish standard operating procedures for the coordination and control of
aircraft between ZLC ARTCC and Big Sky TRACON on the VATSIM network. This letter of
agreement is supplemental to vZLC Facility Policy and FAA Order JO 7110.65. The information
contained herein is to be used for flight simulation purposes only on the VATSIM network. It is
not intended, nor should it be used for, real-world navigation. The Virtual Salt Lake City ARTCC
is not affiliated with the FAA, the actual Salt Lake City ARTCC, or any governing aviation body.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES:

    a. All controllers staffing (or controlling overlying airspace containing) ZLC ARTCC and/or
         Big Sky TRACON positions (not including Bozeman Radar) are required to be familiar
         with the provisions of this document.

    b. ZLC ARTCC delegates responsibility to Big Sky TRACON for providing terminal air traffic
         control services within the airspace depicted in Attachment 1.

4. DEFINITIONS:

    a. Arrival Fix: A fix on the ZLC ARTCC/Big Sky TRACON common airspace boundary for
         use by arrival aircraft, as depicted in Attachment 3, Figure 1, and described in
         Attachment 3, Table 1.

    b. Departure Fix: A fix on the ZLC ARTCC/Big Sky TRACON common airspace boundary
         for use by departing aircraft, as depicted in Attachment 3, Figure 1, and described in
         Attachment 3, Table 2.

    c. Corridor: Areas on the ZLC ARTCC/Big Sky TRACON common airspace boundary used
         by both arrivals and departures, as depicted in Attachment 3, Figure 1, and described in
         Attachment 3, Table 3.

5. GENERAL PROCEDURES:

    a. Big Sky TRACON must keep ZLC ARTCC advised of the runway flow in use.

    b. Each facility must be responsible for any required intra-facility coordination.

    c. Overflight aircraft must be routed as filed.

6. ARRIVAL PROCEDURES:
    a. BOI arrival aircraft must be:
              (1) Assigned an RNAV STAR, or;
              (2) Routed via an arrival fix then direct BOI VORTAC/KBOI, or;
              (3) Routed through the ELLIS corridor direct BOI VORTAC/KBOI, or;
              (4) Routed through the EUUGG corridor direct BOI VORTAC/KBOI.
    b. Satellite airport arrival aircraft must be:
              (1) Routed via an arrival fix, direct BOI, direct destination, or;
              (2) Routed through the ELLIS corridor direct destination, or;
              (3) Routed through the EUUGG corridor, direct BOI, direct destination.
    c. ZLC ARTCC must issue altitudes to arrival aircraft in accordance with the following:
              (1) Aircraft assigned an RNAV STAR must be cleared to descend via the STAR and
                   issued the landing direction.
              (2) Aircraft not assigned an RNAV STAR and routed via an arrival fix:
                        (a) Operating at or above FL180: The aircraft must be assigned 17,000 feet
                             MSL.
                        (b) Operating below 17,000 feet MSL: The aircraft must be level five (5) NM
                             prior to crossing the lateral boundary of Big Sky TRACON airspace.
              (3) Aircraft not assigned an RNAV STAR and routed through the ELLIS or EUUGG
                   corridor: Assigned 17,000 feet MSL.
    d. A handoff and communications transfer of arriving aircraft constitutes a transfer of
         control to Big Sky TRACON for:
              (1) Descent.
              (2) Turns up to 40° either side of the previous course.
              (3) Speed reductions within the confines of the lateral boundaries of Big Sky
                   TRACON airspace.
    e. ZLC ARTCC must handoff aircraft that will enter Big Sky TRACON airspace to the
         appropriate sector, in accordance with Attachment 2.

7. DEPARTURE PROCEDURES:

    a. Big Sky TRACON must provide five (5) NM of separation, constant or increasing, to ZLC
         ARTCC.

    b. Aircraft must be assigned 16,000 feet MSL or the requested altitude, if lower.
    c. Aircraft must be routed via a departure fix and at or above the minimum altitude for the

         fix, in accordance with Attachment 3.
    d. Aircraft that are unable to be routed via a departure fix must be:

              (1) Assigned routing that exits Big Sky TRACON airspace through the ELLIS or
                   EUUGG corridors, or;

              (2) Issued the filed flight plan, if coordinated with ZLC ARTCC.
    e. A handoff and communications transfer of departing aircraft constitutes a transfer of

         control to ZLC ARTCC for turns up to 40° either side of the previous course.
