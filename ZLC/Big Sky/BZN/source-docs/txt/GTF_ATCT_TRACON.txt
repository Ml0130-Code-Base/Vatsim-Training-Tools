                                           Clearance

1. Frequency
         (1) Great Falls Ground: 121.700

2. Procedures
         (1) When appropriate, inform pilots of the current ATIS information prior to contacting
              ground control.

3. IFR Routing
         (1) Initial Altitude: 13,000.
         (2) For all aircraft, issue the following headings for radar vectors to their route,
              dependent on their departure runway:
                   (a) Runway 3, 35 - Heading 030
                   (b) Runway 17, 21 - Heading 210

4. Standard Departure Frequencies
         (1) When appropriate, issue the following frequencies to IFR aircraft:
                   (a) Great Falls Approach 128.6
         (2) When appropriate, issue the following frequencies to VFR aircraft requiring radar
              services after departure:
                   (a) Great Falls Approach 128.6

5. VFR Routing
         (1) Not Applicable.

                                            Ground

1. Frequency
         (1) Great Falls Ground: 121.700

2. Airspace
         (1) Ground: All movement areas except active runways.

3. Procedures
         (1) Not applicable.
4. Departures
         (1) All aircraft requiring pushback onto the movement area will contact ground prior
              to pushback and start-up.
         (2) All aircraft will contact ground with position and ATIS prior to being issued taxi
              instructions.

5. Arrivals
         (1) All aircraft will remain on ground frequency until off the movement area.

                                              Local

1. Frequency
         (1) Great Falls Tower: 118.700

2. Airspace
         (1) Local: Runways 3/21, 17/35 and GTF Class D airspace.

3. Procedures
         (1) Runway operations
                   (a) Use runway 3 (North) during calm wind operations.
                   (b) Use runway 21 (South) when the tailwind component exceeds 5 knots.
         (2) LAHSO
                   (a) Not authorized.
         (3) Tower Use of Radar:
                   (a) Full

4. Departures
         (1) Departure Notification / Releases
                   (a) Departure Notifications are not required, unless otherwise coordinated by
                        TRACON.
                   (b) Departures are automatically released, unless otherwise coordinated by
                        TRACON.
  5. Arrivals

    (1) IFR Arrivals

               (a) Circling procedures N/A

    (2) Missed Approach/Go Around

               (a) Acquire the track control for the aircraft conducting a missed approach.

               (b) Send a Missed Notification (.MA) for the aircraft executing an unplanned
                   Missed Approach/Go Around to the appropriate TRACON sector that will
                   be receiving the aircraft for re-sequencing.

               (c) VFR aircraft, and/or IFR Category I/II aircraft conducting a visual
                   approach and executing a Go-Around must be instructed to remain in the
                   local pattern, if capable of doing so.

               (d) The following is considered Primary Climb-Out procedure and does not
                   require any special coordination with Approach Control or Strip marking
                   (Scratchpad entries) other than the basic Missed Notification. Aircraft
                   must not turn prior to the departure end of the runway.
                   Handoff/Comm-Change to the appropriate TRACON sector must be
                   completed no later than 1 mile off the departure end of the runway.

               (e) CLIMB OUT PROCEDURE:

                 (i) North Flow: Heading 030, Maintain 6,000.

                 (ii) South Flow: Heading 210, Maintain 6,000.

               (f) VFR traffic join local pattern.

  6. VFR Operations

    (1) VFR Departures

               (a) Depart on-course.

               (b) Handoff to departure control as soon as clear of any possible conflicts.

                 (i) Great Falls Approach 128.6

               

    (2) VFR Arrivals

               (a) Direct to airport.

    (3) VFR Flight Following
                   (a) Ensure all aircraft requiring flight following have the appropriate
                        scratchpad entry prior to handoff to departure.
