                                           Clearance

1. Frequency
         (1) Bozeman Clearance: 124.050

2. Procedures
         (1) When appropriate, inform pilots of the current ATIS information prior to contacting
              ground control.

3. IFR Routing
         (1) Initial Altitude: 16,000.
                   (a) When the BGSKY, BOBKT, or MEADO SID has been assigned, the pilot
                        must be instructed to "Climb via SID."
         (2) Route all IFR departures on an approved SID appropriate for their route of flight.
              Aircraft unable/refusing to accept a SID must be assigned radar vectors (see
              3.(3)).
                   (a) East Operations (Runway 12):
                           (i) SW to NW departures issue BGSKY SID. Non-RNAV aircraft
                                  should be issued the HIA SID.
                          (ii) NE to SE departures issue BOBKT SID.
                   (b) West Operations (Runway 30):
                           (i) All directions issue MEADO SID. Non-RNAV aircraft may be
                                  issued the HIA SID.
         (3) If a pilot cannot accept a departure procedure, issue the following headings for
              radar vectors to their route, per their departure runway:
                   (a) Runway 12 - Heading 250
                   (b) Runway 30 - Heading 290

4. Standard Departure Frequencies
         (1) When appropriate, issue the following frequencies to IFR aircraft and VFR aircraft
              requiring radar services after departure:
                   (a) Big Sky 118.975

5. VFR Routing
         (1) Not applicable.
                                            Ground

1. Frequency
         (1) Bozeman Ground: 121.800

2. Airspace
         (1) All movement areas with the exception of active runways.

3. Procedures
         (1) Not applicable.

4. Departures
         (1) All aircraft requiring pushback onto the movement area will contact ground prior
              to pushback and start-up.
         (2) All aircraft will contact ground with position and ATIS prior to being issued taxi
              instructions.
         (3) During periods of high volumes of traffic, the published Standard Runway
              Assignments may be used to expedite the flow of departures.
         (4) Standard Runway Assignments:
                   (a) East Operations
                           (i) Runway 12: All departures.
                   (b) West Operations
                           (i) Runway 30: All departures.

5. Arrivals
         (1) All aircraft will remain on ground frequency until off the movement area.
                                              Local

1. Frequency

         (1) Bozeman Tower (Local): 118.200

2. Airspace

         (1) Local: Runways 3/21, 11/29, 11G/29G, 12/30, and airspace as depicted on sector
              file.

3. Procedures

         (1) Runway operations

                   (a) Use runway 12 (East) during calm wind operations.

                   (b) Use runway 30 (West) when the tailwind component exceeds 5 knots.

         (2) LAHSO

                   (a) The following procedure defines position responsibilities for the use of
                        Land and Hold Short Operations.

                           (i) The LAHSO runway must be dry

                          (ii) The tailwind on the LAHSO runway shall be calm (less than 3
                                  knots)

                         (iii) The RED LAHSO CHIP will be placed face up at the bottom center
                                  of the TMB, when LAHSO is authorized, and placed face down in
                                  the same place when not

                         (iv) A RED magnetic chip will accompany the magnetic chip of the
                                  aircraft which is landing and holding short

                          (v) LAHSO must not be utilized if wind shear has been reported

                         (vi) Ceiling of 1000 feet and 3 miles visibility must be reported at the
                                  airfield

                        (vii) Runway 12 has 6,841 feet available landing distance (ALD) for
                                  LAHSO, when ALD is requested by a Pilot; it will be rounded down
                                  to 6,800 ft. of ALD, IAW JO 7110.65

                        (viii) All other procedures are outlined in FAA JO 711.118

                         (ix) Traffic will be issued in accordance with FAA JO 7110.118 and the
                                  FAA JO 711.65
                   (b) LAHSO for Runway 12 will be limited to:
                           (i) Daytime operations only
                          (ii) Propeller driven aircraft in FAA Group 7 and below

                   (c) The following aircraft WILL NOT be allowed to conduct LAHSO.
                           (i) All Air Carrier and Air Taxi aircraft
                          (ii) All jet aircraft
                         (iii) All solo student pilots
                         (iv) BE60 Beach Duke
                          (v) Super DC-3 (C-117, R4D 8)
                         (vi) HERN Heron DH-114

                        (vii) PAY3 Piper Cheyenne 3
4. Local Radar

         (1) Tower Use of Radar
                   (a) Limited

5. Departures
         (1) Departure Notification / Releases
                   (a) Departure Notifications are not required, unless otherwise coordinated by
                        TRACON.
                   (b) Departures are automatically released, unless otherwise coordinated by
                        TRACON.
         (2) IFR Departures
                   (a) Ensure aircraft turn to assigned point/heading and altitude reporting is
                        operating prior to handoff to departure.
         (3) VFR Departures
                   (a) Handoff to departure control as soon as clear of any possible conflicts.
                   (b) Ensure all aircraft requiring flight following have the appropriate
                        scratchpad entry prior to handoff to departure.
6. Arrivals

         (1) IFR Arrivals

                   (a) N/A

         (2) Missed Approach/Go Around

                   (a) Send a Missed Notification (.MA) for the aircraft executing an unplanned
                        Missed Approach/Go Around to the appropriate TRACON sector that will
                        be receiving the aircraft for re-sequencing.

                   (b) VFR aircraft, and/or IFR Category I/II aircraft conducting a visual
                        approach and executing a Go-Around must be instructed to remain in the
                        local pattern, if capable of doing so.

                   (c) The following is considered Primary Climb-Out procedure and does not
                        require any special coordination with Approach Control or Strip marking
                        (Scratchpad entries) other than the basic Missed Notification. Aircraft
                        must not turn prior to the departure end of the runway.
                        Handoff/Comm-Change to the appropriate TRACON sector must be
                        completed no later than 1 mile off the departure end of the runway.

                   (d) CLIMB OUT PROCEDURE:

                           (i) East Flow: Heading 250, Maintain 8,000.

                          (ii) West Flow: Heading 290, Maintain 8,000.

                   (e) VFR traffic join local pattern.
