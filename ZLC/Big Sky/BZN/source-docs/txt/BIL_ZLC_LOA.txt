Salt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center and Billings Terminal Radar Approach Control

                             LETTER OF AGREEMENT

                                                    EFFECTIVE: May 3, 2025

SUBJECT: Coordination Procedures

1. PURPOSE: This Letter of Agreement establishes procedures for handling air traffic between
Salt Lake City Air Route Traffic Control Center (ZLC ARTCC) and Billings Terminal Radar
Approach Control (BIL TRACON).

2. SCOPE: To establish standard operating procedures for the coordination and control of
aircraft between ZLC ARTCC and BIL TRACON on the VATSIM network. This letter of
agreement is supplemental to vZLC Facility Policy and FAA Order JO 7110.65. The information
contained herein is to be used for flight simulation purposes only on the VATSIM network. It is
not intended, nor should it be used for, real-world navigation. The Virtual Salt Lake City ARTCC
is not affiliated with the FAA, the actual Salt Lake City ARTCC, or any governing aviation body.

3. RESPONSIBILITIES:

    a. All controllers staffing (or controlling overlying airspace containing) ZLC ARTCC and/or
         BIL TRACON positions are required to be familiar with the provisions of this document.

    b. ZLC ARTCC delegates responsibility to BIL TRACON for providing terminal air traffic
         control services within the airspace extending from the surface up to and including
         15,000 feet MSL within 40 NM of a point centered at 45.49.05N 108.32.49W. This
         airspace is depicted in Attachment 1.

4. DEFINITIONS:

    a. Coordination Points: A point at the intersection of the lateral boundary of BIL TRACON
         delegated airspace and the specified radial of the BIL VORTAC. These points (and their
         associated radials) are listed and depicted in Attachment 1.

5. GENERAL PROCEDURES:

    a. A radar handoff and communications transfer constitutes transfer of control for:

              (1) Climb/Descent.

              (2) Turns up to 30° either side of the previous course.

    b. BIL TRACON must keep ZLC ARTCC advised of the runway flow in use.

    c. Minimum radar separation between facilities must be 5 NM, constant or increasing.

    d. Inappropriate altitude for direction of flight (IAFDOF) may be assigned in accordance

6. ARRIVAL PROCEDURES:
    a. ZLC ARTCC must:
              (1) Ensure BIL arrival aircraft are routed via:
                        (a) An RNAV STAR, or;
                        (b) An airway terminating at BIL VORTAC, or;
                        (c) Direct KBIL.

                   NOTE-
                   RNAV-capable aircraft must be assigned an RNAV STAR.

              (2) Assign altitudes to arrival aircraft in accordance with the following:
                        (a) Aircraft assigned an RNAV STAR must be cleared to descend via the
                             STAR and issued the landing direction.
                        (b) Aircraft not assigned an RNAV STAR:
                                (i) Operating at or above 16,000: The aircraft must be assigned
                                       16,000 feet MSL. A pilot's discretion descent is approved.
                               (ii) Operating at or below 15,000: The aircraft must be level ten (10)
                                       NM prior to crossing the lateral boundary.

7. DEPARTURE PROCEDURES:
    a. BIL TRACON must:
              (1) Ensure that, prior to leaving the lateral boundary:
                        (a) Aircraft whose cleared route includes an airway in BIL TRACON's
                             airspace are established on that airway.
                        (b) Aircraft whose cleared route does not include an airway in BIL TRACON's
                             airspace are cleared direct to the first fix on their route that is outside of
                             BIL TRACON's airspace.
              (2) Clear aircraft to 15,000 MSL, or lower requested altitude, until ZLC ARTCC
                   accepts the handoff of that aircraft.
              (3) Clear aircraft to the following altitude when ZLC ARTCC accepts a handoff from
                   BIL TRACON (before transferring communications):
